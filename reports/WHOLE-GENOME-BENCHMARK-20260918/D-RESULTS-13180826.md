The predeclared chr10/20 stratum excludes chromosomes found in D TRAIN/CAL/DEV for each species; it tests new chromosomes in two fine-tuning species, not unseen-species generalization.

Eligible whole-assembly denominators are 1,054,707,601 bp for chicken and 1,597,262,566 bp for zebrafish. The raw result preserves every exclusion count and fixed exposure manifest.
