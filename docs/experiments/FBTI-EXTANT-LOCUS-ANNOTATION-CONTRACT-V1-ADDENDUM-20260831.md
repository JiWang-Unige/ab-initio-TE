# FBTI extant-locus annotation contract V1 addendum

Date: 2026-08-31

Status: **implementation-blocking addendum; no Gate L result yet**

This addendum overrides the conflicting fields and calculations in
`FBTI-EXTANT-LOCUS-ANNOTATION-CONTRACT-V0-20260831.md`. Unchanged V0 clauses
remain in force. The V1 estimand is reproducibility on one preregistered
challenge panel, not prevalence in the full FlyBase population.

## 1. Estimand and claim boundary

The Gate L estimand is conditional on the fixed 120-package main panel:

- 60 S0 packages;
- 60 S1 packages;
- the hard-cell mixture in Section 2;
- exact r6.68 coordinates and 10 kb flanks;
- packages as the independent and bootstrap units.

The panel is deliberately challenge-balanced. Set:

```text
estimand_scope=conditional_challenge_panel
population_representative=false
```

Gate L does not require unit inclusion probabilities or Horvitz--Thompson
weights. No result may be restated as a FlyBase-population prevalence,
population mean, or natural S0/S1 mixture. A population analysis would require
a separate protocol written before examining its outcomes.

## 2. Joint panel freeze

All 172 packages are selected simultaneously and must have no positive-length
overlap. `end0 == start0` is non-overlap.

### S0 hard cells

| Cell | Core length | Main quota |
|---|---:|---:|
| `S0-L1` | `<80` | 15 |
| `S0-L2` | `80--499` | 15 |
| `S0-L3` | `500--999` | 15 |
| `S0-L4` | `>=1000` | 15 |

### S1 hard cells

| Cell | Definition | Main quota |
|---|---|---:|
| `S1-C1` | `feature_count=2` and `max_overlap_depth=2` | 20 |
| `S1-C2` | `feature_count>=3` and `max_overlap_depth=2` | 20 |
| `S1-C3` | `max_overlap_depth>=3` | 20 |

The S1 cells are mutually exclusive and exhaustive.

### Role totals

| Role | S0 | S1 | Cell restriction |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| calibration | 6 | 6 | each S0 cell at least once; each S1 cell exactly twice |
| main | 60 | 60 | exact main quotas above |
| reserve | 20 | 20 | no cell quota |

Calibration is excluded from every Gate L metric. Reserve is frozen before
annotation and is used only as the paired prefix required by L-D.

Use a single label-blind solver objective generated by NumPy `PCG64` with seed
`20260831`. The priority cannot use P3 multiplicity, nearest-FBti gap,
`flybase_name`, an annotation, or a difficulty judgement. These variables are
reported only as post-freeze diagnostics. The panel is never changed after
seeing those diagnostics.

## 3. Runtime input equality

Do not create checksum sidecars. The consumed objects are compared directly.

1. Parse the exact r6.68 FASTA and require exact equality of the full
   `contig_name -> length` mapping to the supplied length table. Count and total
   length alone are insufficient.
2. Require unique truth `feature_id`, registered release/species, legal
   coordinates, and exact row fields consumed by the panel builder.
3. Reconstruct every truth interval-overlap pair and require complete set
   equality with the supplied overlap table.
4. Require legal, unique canonical P3 coordinate tuples. Do not normalize
   contig names, discard unknown contigs, merge atoms, or alter coordinates.
5. Record the Git commit that generated the panel. Later consumers compare
   their actual package/context/atom rows directly with the committed
   manifests.

An input or construction mismatch is `CONTRACT_INVALID`; it is not a biological
Gate L failure and enters no scientific denominator.

## 4. Frozen panel manifests

The package manifest contains at least:

```text
package_id role unit_type hard_cell seqid
core_start0 core_end0 package_start0 package_end0
feature_ids reserve_pair_rank selection_priority
```

For every selected package, `context_features.tsv` contains every FBti record
with positive-length overlap with the package, including non-focal records.

For every selected package, `package_atoms.tsv` contains every canonical P3
atom with positive-length package overlap:

```text
package_id atom_id seqid start0 end0 overlap_role package_censored
```

`atom_id` is `P3:{seqid}:{start0}:{end0}`. An atom crossing a package boundary
is retained and marked censored; it is excluded from L-D, Gate O and Gate E. Because
selected packages do not overlap, an atom cannot belong to two packages.

## 5. Material and locus schema

Observed TE-derived material may be identifiable even when its locus grouping
is not. Therefore `material_segments.tsv` adds:

```text
locus_assignment_status = assigned | unresolved
locus_id = required only when assigned
```

Package-union material IoU uses both assigned and unresolved supported material.
`material_of` uses assigned rows only. An unresolved material segment does not
create a fictitious locus.

All material must lie on the package contig and inside its interval. Assigned
material from two resolved or partially resolved loci cannot overlap in base
space; ambiguous attribution is represented as unresolved assignment.

### Package status

| Status | Operational definition |
|---|---|
| `resolved` | at least one stable locus; all supported material assigned; every declared locus pair has a definite relation |
| `partially_resolved` | at least one stable locus and some assigned material, but a locus partition, assignment, or topology remains unresolved |
| `unresolved` | supported material is present but no stable locus partition can be asserted; zero loci is legal |
| `abstained` | the evidence packet is insufficient; the annotator makes no material or locus assertion |

### Locus status

| Status | Operational definition |
|---|---|
| `resolved` | material membership and immediate parent/distinct relations are stable |
| `partially_resolved` | locus existence is supported but membership or topology remains uncertain |
| `unresolved` | provisional hypothesis only; excluded from locus matching, L-D, O and E |

`abstained` is package-only. A boundary class of `unidentifiable` is a completed
epistemic conclusion and does not by itself downgrade a locus.

## 6. Matching and ontology-edge agreement

Candidate locus matches require supported-material-union IoU `>=0.50`.
Candidate material-segment matches require segment IoU `>=0.50`. Matching is:

1. maximum cardinality;
2. maximum total IoU;
3. canonical coordinate-tuple tie-break.

Actor-local IDs cannot break ties. IoU cutoffs 0.25 and 0.75 may be reported as
non-gating sensitivities.

Rename the four-class statistic to `ontology_edge_macro_f1`. For every
unordered pair of declared loci, exactly one relation is required:

```text
nested_in(i,j)
nested_in(j,i)
distinct_locus(i,j)
overlap_unresolved(i,j)
```

`nested_in` stores only the immediate parent; transitive ancestry is derived.
The active edge-type set is fixed once on the complete main panel.

`interruptions.tsv` adds `child_locus_id`. It is required only for
`nested_locus_occupied` and must agree with one immediate `nested_in` edge.

## 7. Boundary-identifiability agreement

Keep nominal three-class Gwet AC1 for `point`, `interval`, and
`unidentifiable`, with the registered threshold `>=0.60`. It is evaluable only
with at least 40 matched locus pairs from at least 20 main packages. Otherwise
the status is `LABEL_DENOMINATOR_INSUFFICIENT_BOUNDARY_STATUS`, not AC1 zero and
not an L-R biological failure.

AC1 supports agreement on the epistemic class only. It does not support a claim
that two boundary coordinate intervals agree.

## 8. Pass-2 atom projection

For atom `a`, let `C` be the fraction of its bases overlapping any assigned
supported material. Apply this precedence:

1. atom not fully inside the package: `projection_eligibility=package_censored`;
2. allowed boundary uncertainty changes assignment: `unresolved`;
3. `C < 0.50`: `unassigned`;
4. top locus has at least 90% of supported overlap and second locus at most 10%:
   `unique`;
5. at least two loci each cover at least 20% of the atom length: `mixed`;
6. all remaining cases: `unresolved`.

Package-censored atoms are reported but excluded from L-D, O and E.

## 9. L-D denominator distribution

| Quantity | Minimum |
|---|---:|
| resolved multipart loci | 30 from at least 20 packages |
| nested relations | 20 from at least 10 packages; parent and child each have an eligible unique atom |
| distinct-locus hard-negative pairs | 30 from at least 15 packages; each locus has an eligible unique atom |
| positive co-locus atom pairs | 50 from at least 25 packages |
| eligible mixed/unresolved atoms | 15 from at least 10 packages |

Package-censored atoms do not contribute. This prevents a single large S1
component from satisfying L-D by itself.

Reserve ranks add one S0 and one S1 package together. Activated reserve affects
L-D only; it does not change L-R, the boundary-copy audit, deep audit, or a
population estimate.

## 10. Bootstrap and status precedence

Use 10,000 replicates and seed `20260831`. Preserve the fixed challenge mixture
within every replicate by sampling with replacement:

- 15 from each S0 main cell;
- 20 from each S1 main cell.

Never resample atom pairs. If an active ontology-edge type has zero denominator
in one replicate, its F1 is zero for that replicate; do not remove the type.
Repeatedly sampled packages contribute with their bootstrap multiplicity.

Final Gate L status order is:

1. malformed input/schema/construction: `CONTRACT_INVALID`;
2. true, evaluable provenance failure: `NO_GO_LP`;
3. any evaluable reproducibility metric below threshold: `NO_GO_LR`;
4. an unevaluable required L-R metric: its explicit denominator-insufficient status;
5. L-P/L-R pass but L-D short before all reserve pairs: `INCOMPLETE`;
6. L-P/L-R pass but L-D short after all reserve pairs:
   `LABEL_DENOMINATOR_INSUFFICIENT`;
7. all registered checks pass: `PASS`.

An observed L-R failure takes precedence over a separate denominator shortfall.

## 11. Exact-name and future Gate E inputs

Full `flybase_name` is retained only as raw provenance. Remove exact-name
frequency from sampling and split rules. Report the old exact-name-matched FPR
as `NOT_ESTIMABLE_ZERO_DENOMINATOR`; it is not a pass.

If L and O pass, Gate E may replace it with an explicitly registered
RC-invariant local-sequence-similarity and distance-matched different-locus
safety analysis. This is a sequence-similarity control, not a family label.

Frozen P3 atoms have no probability values. L and O do not require them. No
probability export or neural inference is authorized by this addendum; Gate E
must first receive a separate input/resource amendment after L and O pass.

## 12. Joint-panel stop rule

Run the fixed-seed exact joint selection once. Stop with
`PROTOCOL_INFEASIBLE_OR_INPUT_MISMATCH` if either:

1. direct runtime equality of the consumed assembly mapping, truth table,
   overlap graph, or committed manifest fails; or
2. the solver cannot satisfy all role totals, main hard-cell quotas,
   calibration coverage and pairwise non-overlap for 172 packages.

Do not change the seed or priority, swap packages, use greedy repair, shorten
flanks, or relax a quota. Any retry requires a versioned protocol amendment
that states exactly which contract changed.
