# P3-R2 boundary-decomposition decision (2026-08-29)

## Frozen interpretation

P3-R1 is a supervised Human comparator-label experiment, not self-supervised
post-training and not a cross-species result.  It trained on 3,000 Human chr1
windows, validated on 800 Human chr11 windows, and evaluated on 1,200 Human
chr17 windows (8192 bp each, seed 42, 800 steps).  Its Human chr17 results were:

- bp F1 0.934496
- segment F1 at IoU 0.8 0.378631
- boundary F1 at 5 bp 0.215650
- short-prediction rate 0.551110
- fragments/truth 1.292430
- missed rate 0.055637

The R1 sample is adequate for an engineering/mechanism pilot, but one seed and
one Human chromosome split are not adequate for a final species-level claim.

The three GPU allocations have a combined ceiling of 22 GPU-hours.  A CPU
preflight must first prove that every train/validation window can construct the
true and matched-control targets without changing the sample denominator.

## Gates

P3-R2 route gate on Human chr17:

- short-prediction rate <= 0.497931
- fragments/truth <= 1.180148
- segment F1 >= 0.378631 and > Base 0.339956
- boundary F1 at 5 bp > 0.215650; > DAPT 0.224286 is preferred
- boundary recall/F1 at 25 bp improves
- bp F1 drop relative to P3-R1 <= 0.005
- missed-rate increase relative to Base <= 0.03

Aligned-boundary mechanism gate versus the shuffled control:

- boundary F1 at 5 bp improves by at least 0.01
- segment F1 improves by at least 0.01
- fragments/truth is at least 5% lower

## Decision tree agreed with ChatGPT Pro

1. If the Human route gate fails, or P3-R2 is indistinguishable from the
   shuffled control, stop direct-annotation P3 development and move to HiTE-like
   multi-copy hybrid discovery/refinement.
2. Only if the Human route gate passes and aligned P3-R2 beats the control may a
   balanced multi-chromosome Human confirmatory run be started.
3. P3-X changes claim scope, not the route decision: Mouse and FlyBase gains
   support preliminary transfer; Mouse-only gain limits the claim to nearer
   mammals; neither gain makes the result Human/comparator-specific.

No pilot outcome supports claims of full-length biological TE recovery,
intact-insertion discovery, unseen-family discovery, de novo discovery, or
universal TE annotation.  Those require independent structural truth and/or a
hybrid multi-copy validation stage.
