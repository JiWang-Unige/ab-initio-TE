# P3-R2 mechanism and transfer closure

Date: 2026-08-30

## Decision

The frozen Human route and mechanism gates both failed. Pure direct P3 is
closed. A balanced multi-chromosome Human confirmation, P2-HM, further
boundary-loss or threshold tuning, MoE and a TE-specific HMM are not
authorized. The subsequent C5-H hybrid pilot also stopped at A1 because P3,
HiTE and union failed to establish a usable multi-copy substrate under the
frozen search contract. A2/A3 and C5-F are not authorized; see
[`C5-HYBRID-CLOSURE-20260831.md`](C5-HYBRID-CLOSURE-20260831.md).

ChatGPT Pro independently reviewed the completed results and reached the same
decision. Its narrow interpretation is that the matched shuffled auxiliary
task outperformed coordinate-aligned boundary supervision; the observed gain
therefore cannot be attributed to learning RepeatMasker reference-run boundary
positions.

## Human P3-R2 mechanism result

Both arms used the same Human chr1 train (3,000 windows), chr11 validation
(800 windows) and chr17 test (1,200 windows), 8192-bp geometry, backbone,
U-Net trunk, body targets, seed 42, 800 training steps, threshold and test
denominator (`14,253` true chr17 segments). The only scientific difference was
the left/right boundary target:

- **Aligned:** true triangular targets within +/-16 bp of the comparator-run
  edge.
- **Matched control:** a deterministic joint permutation of `(left, right)`
  target pairs over the same boundary-valid positions in each window. It
  preserves the body labels, valid mask, per-head target-value multiset,
  target mass, non-zero count and left/right pair distribution while breaking
  spatial alignment and local triangular continuity.

### Frozen mechanism gate

All three aligned-versus-control conditions failed in the wrong direction:

| Condition | Required aligned advantage | Observed aligned minus control | Verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Segment F1@0.8 | >=+0.01 absolute | -0.040004 | **FAIL** |
| Boundary F1@5 | >=+0.01 absolute | -0.034969 | **FAIL** |
| Fragments/truth | >=5% relative lower | 9.11% relative higher | **FAIL** |

The appropriate conclusion is not that random boundaries are biologically
better. The result shows that aligned triangular supervision on these
RepeatMasker-style run edges did not supply useful instance-boundary
information under this recipe. Plausible explanations include noisy
comparator-run edges, conflict with body continuity, or regularization from
the auxiliary training geometry. The experiment does not distinguish these
explanations.

## External P3-R1 diagnostics

These evaluations used the frozen Human P3-R1 checkpoint, not P3-R2. They
change claim scope but cannot rescue the failed Human route.

The contiguous 1,200-window Mouse chr1 panel produced:

- bp F1: 0.902004
- bp recall: 0.952980
- segment F1@0.8: 0.096592
- segment recall@0.8: 0.306523
- boundary F1@5: 0.026062
- boundary recall@5: 0.082704
- boundary F1@25: 0.072615

- missed rate: 0.056963
- short rate: approximately 0.851

This is an absolute transfer diagnostic because no matched Mouse Base/DAPT

signal can remain detectable while instance reconstruction and boundary
localization degrade severely.

## Evidence classification

- **Scientific route-selection evidence:** the same-denominator Human aligned
  versus matched-control result and its frozen gates.
- **External diagnostics:** Mouse T0 absolute transfer and FlyBase T1
  positive-only recovery.
- **Engineering pilot evidence:** serial-to-sharded exact FlyBase inference,
  output conversion and dependency recovery.
- **Not established:** biological insertion boundaries, full-copy accuracy,
  unseen-family discovery, de novo discovery, whole-genome FlyBase precision,
  or broad cross-species annotation.

## Closed directions

- Generic Human MLM-DAPT and the tested TE-aware span-MLM recipe as fragment
  solutions.
- The current pure direct P3 escalation ladder, including balanced Human
  confirmation.
- Further boundary-loss, threshold, context or decoder tuning on this route.
- P2-HM/P4, MoE and a TE-specific HMM in the next round.
- Repackaging gap merging, minimum-length filtering, HMM/CRF inference
  smoothing or other already-failed post-processing.

## Completed next route: C5-H hybrid information-gain pilot

### Hypothesis

Target-genome multi-copy evidence plus seed-only consensus refinement provides
instance-level information missing from local direct inference, improving
segment and boundary recovery and reducing fragmentation without materially
losing recall.

### Frozen Human data contract

- Human chr17:0-9,830,400, the same comparator-strict truth, callable mask and
  evaluator.
- Seed-selection rules are frozen on chr11 validation or tool-native score
  distributions without consulting chr17 truth.
- Candidate search uses only the target genome. Dfam, Repbase and other known
  TE libraries are excluded from the discovery/refinement arms.

### Seed sources and four required stages

Run the same four stages for frozen P3-R1 seeds, HiTE seeds and their union:

| Stage | Intervention | Question answered |
|---|---|---|
| A0 | Seed-only predictions | Frozen baseline |
| A1 | Target-genome copy search only | Does repeated-copy evidence add information? |
| A2 | A1 plus multi-copy alignment and flank-divergence boundary refinement | Does the proposed boundary mechanism add value? |
| A3 | A2 plus a consensus built only from seeds and retrieved copies, followed by short-fragment recovery | Does consensus recovery add recall without restoring fragmentation? |

Report the existing strict Human metrics plus copies retrieved per seed and
the fraction of seeds with at least two independent homologous copies.

### Frozen C5-H gate

A2/A3 relative to A0 must satisfy all of:

- segment F1@0.8: +>=0.05 absolute;
- boundary F1@5: +>=0.03 absolute;
- fragments/truth: >=20% relative reduction;
- short rate: >=20% relative reduction;
- bp recall: >=95% of A0;
- missed-rate increase: <=0.03 absolute.

Mechanism requirements:

- A2 must beat A1 by >=0.02 in boundary F1@5 or segment F1;
- A3 must improve bp recall or segment recall over A2 while worsening
  fragments/truth by no more than 10%.

If only A1 improves, the result is homology expansion rather than boundary
refinement. If A3 adds no recovery, remove the consensus-recovery module. If
no seed source passes, stop the hybrid route instead of adding another direct
decoder.

### Conditional C5-F

Only a C5-H GO permits exact FlyBase r6.68 C5-F. FlyBase curated truth remains
evaluation-only. Report positive-truth bp/segment/boundary recall,
fragments/truth, missed and split rates; precision/F1 remain forbidden.

C5-F requires, relative to its seed-only arm:

- segment recall@0.8 and boundary recall@5 each at least 2x;
- fragments/truth at least 20% lower;
- bp recall loss no greater than 5% relative;
- final segment recall@0.8 at least 0.10.

## Publication position

The defensible current result is a mechanism-level negative finding: explicit
coordinate-aligned supervision on RepeatMasker-style run edges did not solve
TE instance fragmentation, and a distribution-matched shuffled control did
better. Mouse and FlyBase show that local TE-associated signal can transfer
without intact instance reconstruction. C5 then failed its frozen A1 substrate
condition across P3, HiTE and union seeds. The publication therefore closes as
a controlled negative benchmark/mechanism diagnosis rather than a positive
hybrid method. The C5 conclusion is limited to the frozen seed definitions and
matched minimap2 contract; it does not reject all multi-copy methods.
